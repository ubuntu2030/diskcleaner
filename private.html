Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Photo Cleaner: Storage Sweep (“the App,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use our Android application. The App helps you analyze storage, find similar photos and videos, compress media, manage files on your device, and optionally browse protected locations with root read-only access.

We designed the App for local processing: scanning, hashing, grouping, compression, and cleanup run on your device. We do not operate application servers that collect your photos, videos, files, contacts, or location. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. Please also read our Terms of Service.

Summary

  • No account or login is required.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Your media and files are processed on-device; we do not upload your library to our servers.
  • Network access is used only for Google Play Billing (purchases, subscriptions, and restore).
  • No ads and no third-party tracking for advertising.
  • Google Play processes payment data under Google’s own privacy policy.

Information We Do Not Collect on Our Servers

We do not operate backend services that receive or store the following from you:

  • Photos, videos, audio, documents, or other file contents
  • File paths, folder structures, or storage scan results
  • Similarity groups, perceptual hashes, or compression outputs
  • Contacts, call logs, SMS, precise or coarse location, or calendar data
  • Advertising identifiers used for cross-app tracking
  • Crash or usage analytics sent to our servers (we do not operate such pipelines)

For Google Play’s Data safety form, the App is intended to be declared as no data collected and no data shared by the developer for the App’s own services, except as described for purchases handled entirely by Google Play.

Information Stored on Your Device

To provide features, the App may store small amounts of data locally on your phone or tablet, including:

  • Preferences: theme, font size, date format, whether to show hidden files, sort order memory, and similar UI settings (SharedPreferences).
  • Scan caches: fingerprints or metadata used to speed up similar photo and video grouping; stored in a local database on the device.
  • Free-tier counters: local counts for daily or batch limits on compression and similar-media cleanup when you are not a Pro subscriber.
  • Pro status cache: a local flag reflecting whether Google Play reports an active subscription or lifetime purchase, so Pro features can work offline after verification.

This data stays on your device unless you uninstall the App, clear app storage, or delete it through your device settings. Uninstalling removes app-private storage; your personal files in shared storage are not deleted unless you delete them inside the App.

The App requests Android permissions only for features you use. Denying a permission may limit a feature but does not block the entire App.

How Features Use Your Data (Locally)

  • Quick Clean & similar media: reads media metadata and content on-device to find duplicates and similar items; deletion uses the system recycle bin when available.
  • Storage analysis: aggregates sizes and categories locally.
  • Compression: reads and writes files you select; output stays on your device unless you move or share it.
  • Apps list: uses standard package visibility (launcher apps) to show installed applications and storage impact—no QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES.
  • Receive / open files: if another app sends a file to Photo Cleaner: Storage Sweep, processing happens locally (e.g., save copy).

Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel in Google Play. Trial length, if offered, is shown on Google Play before you confirm (the App may reference a trial such as 3 days when configured in Play Console).

Third Parties

  • Google Play Services / Billing: purchase and subscription management.
  • Open-source libraries (e.g., image loading, UI components) bundled in the App; they run locally and are not used by us to collect personal data on our servers.

We do not integrate advertising networks or third-party analytics that profile you across apps.

Security

Because processing is local, protecting your device (screen lock, avoiding untrusted root mods, keeping Android updated) is important. We use Android platform APIs for scoped storage, recycle-bin deletion where supported, and FileProvider for safer sharing. No security method is perfect; use the App at your discretion when deleting or compressing files.
